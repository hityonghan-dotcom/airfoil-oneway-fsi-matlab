# Included example media

`transient_flow_alpha_8.mp4` is a 121-frame, 15 fps transient-flow demonstration at `alpha = 8 deg`.

`oneway_motion_alpha_8.mp4` is a 481-frame, 20 fps four-panel one-way-FSI demonstration. Its panels are:

1. Full fixed-geometry CFD field with a moving structural overlay.
2. Enlarged moving airfoil section referenced to its undeformed shape.
3. Actual, unamplified tip displacement and twist histories.
4. CFD lift and drag histories passed to the structural model.

The enlarged section is visual-only: displacement is magnified by 300 and twist by 60. The flow remains a frozen fixed-geometry CFD field, as required by the one-way coupling assumption.
